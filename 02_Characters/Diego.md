# Diego

## Core Identity
- **Full Name:** Diego (Family name rarely emphasized early on)
- **Age:** 14
- **Role:** Chaos Catalyst / Hidden Pathfinder / Future Essential Support
- **Realm Status:** Early-stage cultivation, undefined path
- **Public Impression:** Loud, unserious, constantly hungry, easily distracted
- **True Nature:** Instinct-driven anomaly tracker tied to misplaced structure and hidden paths

## Summary
Diego appears to be disruptive, impulsive, and unserious—but this is only the surface. Beneath that behavior lies a rare sensitivity to inconsistencies in the world around him. He instinctively notices what others overlook: gaps, weak points, hidden alignments, and things that don't quite belong.

He is not strong in a traditional sense, nor particularly disciplined, but he possesses an intuitive understanding of movement through irregular systems. Where others train to follow structure, Diego navigates what breaks it.

His usefulness is not obvious at first.

Then it becomes impossible to ignore.

## Physical Description
- Average build, slightly softer than Tomas
- Always appears relaxed or leaning
- Moves casually, but rarely trips or missteps when it matters
- Eyes constantly shifting—observing more than people realize

## Personality

### Surface Traits
- Loud
- Sarcastic
- Easily distracted
- Constantly eating
- Talks more than necessary
- Pushes boundaries without thinking

### Internal Traits
- Observant in unconventional ways
- Adaptable rather than structured
- Extremely difficult to predict
- Not as careless as he appears
- Has a natural instinct for survival

### Hidden Pattern
Diego often:
- notices things too quickly
- dismisses serious moments with humor
- moves toward "wrong places"
- solves problems accidentally—but consistently

## Strengths
- Environmental awareness (non-traditional)
- Pattern disruption recognition
- High adaptability in unstable conditions
- Fast reaction in unexpected situations
- Extremely hard to corner or trap

## Weaknesses
- Lacks discipline
- Poor structured training habits
- Struggles to explain his instincts
- Distracted by irregularities
- Not physically dominant in straight combat

## Bloodline

### Characteristics
