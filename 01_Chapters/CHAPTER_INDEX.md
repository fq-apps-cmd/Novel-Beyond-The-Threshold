# Chapter Index (Chapters 1–12)

## Chapter 1 — The God Who Chose to Fall

**Mateo Age:** Newborn  
**Realm / Cultivation Stage:** Pre-mortal (cosmic consciousness)  
**Timeline:** Before incarnation  
**Purpose:**
- Establish his former existence, his boredom with absolute power, his fascination with worlds below
- Introduce the idea that perseverance matters as much as talent
- Establish that he moved the beings of his sealed realm into reality rather than destroying them

**Key Events:**
- Mateo observes worlds rising and falling
- Sees underestimated people become legends
- Sees geniuses become mediocre after ascension
- Decides to dismantle himself and become mortal
- Is reborn in the Estrella family

**Important Character Moments:**
- Mateo's decision to become mortal (philosophical turning point)
- First appearance of the sealed realm inhabitants
- Connection to the Estrella lineage established

**Worldbuilding Added:**
- Sealed realm concept
- Ascension philosophy
- Variation in fate and talent across worlds
- Multiple realm tiers exist

**Systems Introduced / Referenced:**
- Ascension mechanics
- Realm tiers and hierarchy
- Sealed realm system

**Seeds for Future Chapters:**
- Estrella mystery begins
- Why certain beings recognize his name
- What the sealed realm contains
- Why he chose this specific family
