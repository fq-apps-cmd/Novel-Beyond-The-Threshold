## Foundation Rule (Critical)

**All six systems must advance in harmony**:

1. **Body** - Physical form, strength, durability
2. **Blood** - Life essence, vitality, power carrier
3. **Meridians** - Cultivation pathways, energy channels
4. **Bones** - Structural integrity, power foundation
5. **Organs** - Internal systems, processing
6. **Soul** - Spirit, consciousness, true self

**Imbalanced cultivation**:
- Creates cracks in foundation
- Can lead to cultivation deviation
- Limits breakthrough potential
- May cause catastrophic failure at realm transition

**Mateo's approach**: Perfect harmony above all else

## Mateo's Cultivation Philosophy

1. **No rushed breakthroughs** - Every transition is intentional
2. **Refinement over speed** - Quality > Quantity
3. **Flawless transitions preferred** - Clean, stable growth
4. **Foundation is sacred** - Perfect base for all higher cultivation
5. **Pressure and refinement** - Enjoys the challenge of proper cultivation
