## Labyrinth System

### Nature
- Not just a dungeon → it is a thinking system

### Behaviors
- Adjusts to movement and intention
- Uses:
  - moving walls
  - perception distortion
  - pressure fields
  - conceptual misalignment
- "Teaches" through:
  - first mistake → warning
  - second mistake → correction/punishment

### Key Mechanics
- Fake paths (not false, just incomplete)
- Rotating / shifting spaces
- Pressure lines (invisible structure traps)
- Responds to awareness level

### Hidden Element (IMPORTANT)
- There is a powerful entity deep in labyrinth
- Mateo:
  - does **NOT** know about it
  - only feels something is "off"
- Entity:
  - watching him
  - amused
  - sees him as interesting
