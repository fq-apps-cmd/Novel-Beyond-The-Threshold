## Sample Entries

### Body Foundation
- **Layer Count:** 10 (Layers 0–9)
- **Entry Layer:** 0
- **Peak Layer:** 9
- **Main Focus:** Strengthening the physical body; building a stable foundation for qi
- **Body Changes:** Muscles and bones temper; skin toughens; stamina increases drastically
- **Meridian Changes:** Meridians begin to open; first pathways become active
- **Breakthrough Risks:** Qi deviation if body is not strong enough; internal injuries
- **Average Age Range:** 8–20 (varies by talent)
- **Forced Ascension Risk:** Low — body must be fully tempered before qi floods in
