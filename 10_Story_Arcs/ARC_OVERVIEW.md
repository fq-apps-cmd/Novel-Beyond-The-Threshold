## Arc Structure Notes

- Arcs may overlap in chapter numbering as multiple storylines progress
- Each arc should have clear beginning, middle, and end
- Mysteries should expand slowly across arcs
- Character development should feel earned, not rushed
- Humor should emerge naturally, not break tension
