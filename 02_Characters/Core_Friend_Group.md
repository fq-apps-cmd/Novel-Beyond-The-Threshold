## Why This Group Works

Each member fills a gap no one else can:

- When Mateo needs someone to **not notice** what he's doing → Diego's chaos covers it
- When Mateo needs to **know what's coming** → Inés sees it first
- When the team needs to **hold the line** → Lucía anchors
- When Mateo needs **tools or traps built** → Rafael crafts it
- When silence and misdirection are needed → Valen disappears into shadow
- When the terrain is the enemy → Kael reads it like a map

Together they form a group that **looks like ordinary village kids** and **fights like a hidden elite unit**.
