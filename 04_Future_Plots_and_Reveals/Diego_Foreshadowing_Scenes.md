# Five Subtle Foreshadowing Scenes — Diego & Mateo

These are meant to be quietly inserted across chapters.

---

## Scene 1 — "The Wrong Path"
*While traveling in the forest:*

Diego stops mid-step.
"…This path is wrong."
Tomas frowns. "What do you mean?"
Diego shrugs. "…It feels like it was moved."
Mateo glances at the ground.
The path… was shifted.

---

## Scene 2 — "Hidden Compartment"
*At the village:*

Diego casually leans against a wall.
"…This part is hollow."
No one reacts.
Mateo taps it later.
Hidden storage.

---

## Scene 3 — "Herb Displacement"
*During a gathering mission:*

Diego frowns at a patch of herbs.
"…These aren't supposed to be here."
Tomas responds: "They're growing fine."
Mateo crouches.
The soil composition is wrong.

---

## Scene 4 — "The Door That Isn't Closed"
*In a ruin:*

Diego walks up to a sealed wall.
"…This is rude."
Tomas blinks. "What?"
Diego presses it.
It opens.

---

## Scene 5 — "Pocket Space Reaction"
*Later:*

Diego glances at Mateo.
"…You're carrying something weird."
Mateo pauses.
Diego shrugs. "…It doesn't sit right."
