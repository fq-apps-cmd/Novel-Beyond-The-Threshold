Doña Reyes might detect such a fluctuation if she happened to be focusing in the relevant direction at the moment of advancement, or she might not — the  
forest's natural energy variability produced flares regularly, and most would not draw her specific attention.  
But Mateo's breakthrough would not produce a detectable fluctuation.

Not Doña Reyes, whose monitoring would register nothing because there would be nothing to register.  
Not the council, whose meeting the next morning would proceed without any new information about Mateo's status.

Until Mateo chose to reveal it.  
Or until circumstances forced revelation.  
Whichever arrived first.

Mateo led them northeast.  
Toward the location he had selected.  
Toward the breakthrough that the night would produce.
