## Summary

Tomas is disciplined, focused, and driven in a way that contrasts sharply with Mateo's relaxed and often playful demeanor. Where Mateo experiments, Tomas commits. Where Mateo questions, Tomas endures. He believes in hard work, repetition, and effort as the path forward.

However, beneath that effort lies a fear: that no matter how hard he tries, he will always fall short — not due to effort, but due to limitation.

That belief is both his weakness… and what will make his eventual rise incredible.

Mateo sees what others don't:
> Tomas is not weak. He is **unrefined**.
